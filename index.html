You know what you want to say. Then the timer starts.

Your mind goes blank, or you talk for too long without making your point. Your structure disappears, and the buzzer sounds before you reach your best idea.

That does not mean you are not suited to Medicine or Dentistry. It usually means you have not yet practised a method for thinking out loud, in a short time, with someone watching.

Why most MMI advice does not help

"Be yourself." "Stay calm." "Show empathy." It is true, but it does not tell you what to do in the first 20 seconds of a station. So strong applicants still:

  • Memorise answers that sound robotic when the question changes
  • Ramble before reaching the central issue
  • Give generic answers that any applicant could give
  • Forget empathy in role-play stations
  • Skip timed practice until interview day

These are skills, not personality traits, and skills can be practised.
